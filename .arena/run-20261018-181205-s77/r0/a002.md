# CakeToHome ERP — Complete System-Wide Audit Architecture & Verification Strategy
## Candidate a002: Dialectical Synthesis + Requirements Checklist + Maximal Rigour

### 1. Dialectical Synthesis: Wide Surface vs. Deep Forensic Integrity
- **Thesis (Broad Surface Audit):** A comprehensive ERP audit must scan every frontend route, every navigation tab, every API endpoint, and every role dashboard to discover orphans, 404s, broken links, and UX dead-ends across the entire surface.
- **Antithesis (Deep Forensic Integrity):** Surface testing is meaningless if underlying double-entry ledgers, atomic drawer locks, concurrency limits, or statutory tax calculations are invalid. Only database-level transaction inspection and mathematical proof guarantee true ERP health.
- **Synthesis:** An integrated two-tier audit combining:
  1. Automated route-by-route and endpoint-by-endpoint enumeration (covering all 10 roles and 50+ routes), coupled with
  2. Deep transaction-level and mathematical verification in PostgreSQL (checking journal vouchers, row-locks, tax snapshots, subledger reconciliations).
