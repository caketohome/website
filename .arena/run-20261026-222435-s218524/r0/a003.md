# Proposal a003: First Principles & Built-To-Last Sequence Governance

## 1. Statutory Invariants
- Invariant 1: Invoice series allocation is a statutory act under CGST Act Section 31 & Rule 46.
- Invariant 2: Sequences are irrevocably bound to legal identities (GSTIN).
- Invariant 3: Software cannot infer or auto-resolve conflicting legal series.

## 2. Architecture Architecture
- Unified pre-flight check in TypeScript service + stored function validation in PostgreSQL.
- Deterministic error returns that prevent silent swallowing.
- Explicit retry queue integration.
