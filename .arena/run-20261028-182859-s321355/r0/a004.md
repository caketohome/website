# Multi-Expert Forensic Architecture and Application Restoration (Plan a004)

## 1. Expert Panel Assessment

### Lead Database Architect
"The PostgreSQL database `cth_test` currently holds 40 tables in `public`. Twenty contain live business and financial records, while 20 are empty. The empty tables are a mix of enterprise ledger tables (e.g. `accounting_journal_entry`) and obsolete prototyping scaffolds (e.g. `order_sequence`, `cms_page`). Dropping tables must be restricted to unreferenced prototypes. Most critically, 26 synthetic audit cakes were written directly to `public.product` during integration tests without cleaning up. These pollute storefront queries because `deleted_at IS NULL`."

### Senior Financial Auditor
"Historical financial integrity is non-negotiable. All 123 orders, 71 invoices, 7 invoice sequence counters, and 98 accounting accounts must remain intact. The statutory invoice sequence fail-closed safeguard must remain active until legal authorization. Furthermore, the admin dashboard displaying `29AAAAA0000A1Z5` is an unverified placeholder that must be reconciled with the active GSTIN registration in the database."

### Storefront & Merchandising Lead
"Customer trust is broken when a customer browses artisanal cakes and sees test products like 'DB PERSISTENCE TEST' or accessories like the 'Gold Acrylic Topper' showing a chocolate cake photograph. The storefront catalog must be reconciled to the authentic 67-item master dump, fixing thumbnail and gallery URLs, isolating categories, and ensuring search relevance."

## 2. Explicit Trade-Off Analysis

| Architectural Decision | Chosen Path | Trade-Off & Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **Synthetic Product Disposition** | Purge unreferenced test cakes; soft-delete referenced test cakes (`deleted_at = NOW()`) | Retains referential integrity for test orders while achieving 100% storefront cleanliness |
| **Empty Table Cleanup** | Drop only 6 obsolete scaffolds (`order_sequence`, `popup_campaigns`, `popup_versions`, `cms_homepage_section`, `cms_page`, `setting`) | Keeps schema clean while preserving future enterprise ERP and accounting ledger schemas |
| **GSTIN Reconciliation** | Dynamically query active GSTIN from `public.gst_registration` | Eliminates sandbox placeholder while preserving fail-closed invoicing safeguards |
| **Image Resolution** | Reconcile against verified assets in `scratch/product_master_dump.json` | Restores authentic photos without inventing unverified stock images |
