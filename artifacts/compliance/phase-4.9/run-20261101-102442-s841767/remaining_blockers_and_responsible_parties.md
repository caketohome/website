# Remaining Blockers and Responsible Parties

| Blocker ID | Description | Responsible Party | Required Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **BLK-001** | Unadopted Board Submission `CTH-GOV-2026-SUB-01` | Board of Directors | Convene meeting and formally adopt resolution. |
| **BLK-002** | Designation of Invoice Prefix (`CTHI` vs `C2H`) | Board of Directors | Specify approved prefix in resolution text. |
| **BLK-003** | Delegation of Statutory Signatories | Board of Directors | Formally appoint Director and CA signatories. |
| **BLK-004** | Review of 3 Historical Delivered Orders | Chartered Accountant | Sign off on write-off or supplementary billing. |
