## 1. Verified Primary Statutory Sources

### 1. Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 — Section 31 (Tax Invoice)
- **Authority:** Ministry of Law and Justice, Government of India.
- **Published:** The Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 12, April 12, 2017.
- **Effective Date:** July 1, 2017.
- **Key Provision (Section 31(1)):** A registered person supplying taxable goods shall, before or at the time of removal of goods for supply to the recipient, issue a tax invoice showing the description, quantity and value of goods, the tax charged thereon and such other particulars as may be prescribed.
- **Prescribed Rules:** Particulars of the tax invoice are prescribed under Rule 46 of the Central Goods and Services Tax Rules, 2017.

### 2. Central Goods and Services Tax Rules, 2017 — Rule 46 (Tax Invoice)
- **Authority:** Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC), Department of Revenue, Ministry of Finance.
- **Notification:** Notification No. 10/2017 - Central Tax, dated June 28, 2017, as amended.
- **Official CBIC Resource:** [https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content-page/explore-rules/1000136/1000001](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content-page/explore-rules/1000136/1000001)
- **Rule 46(b) Verbatim Text:**
  > *(b) a consecutive serial number not exceeding sixteen characters, in one or multiple series, containing alphabets or numerals or special characters- hyphen or dash and slash symbolised as "-" and "/" respectively, and any combination thereof, unique for a financial year;*

### 3. Companies Act, 2013 — Section 134 (Financial statement, Board's report, etc.)
- **Authority:** Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA), Government of India.
- **Published:** The Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, August 30, 2013.
- **Official Resource:** [https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/2114/3/a2013-18.pdf](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/2114/3/a2013-18.pdf)
- **Key Analysis:** Section 134 deals with Board approval of financial statements, Director Responsibility Statements, and internal financial controls. It does **not** specify or mandate Board approval for tax invoice serial prefixes. Invoice prefix designation is an internal corporate governance decision under Internal Financial Controls (IFC).
