# Corporate Authorization Legal Basis Review
**Statutory Review:** Section 134 Companies Act 2013 vs CGST Rule 46  

---

## 1. Clarification of Legal Authority
1. **Companies Act Section 134 Scope:**
   - Mandates approval of financial statements by the Board of Directors and the establishment of adequate Internal Financial Controls (IFC).
   - Does **not** contain any statutory clause mandating that an invoice numbering prefix be approved via formal Board Resolution.
2. **Corporate Governance Designation:**
   - Designating the official prefix (e.g. `CTH`) is an internal management/board policy decision to maintain commercial consistency and internal control.
3. **Role of Chartered Accountant:**
   - An independent Chartered Accountant acts in an advisory and audit capacity.
   - A CA cannot formally approve an operational invoice series in software unless specifically designated as an authorized signatory / Principal Officer by Board delegation.
