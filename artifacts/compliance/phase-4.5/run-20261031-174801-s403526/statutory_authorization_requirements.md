# Statutory Authorization Requirements Specification
**Jurisdiction:** Republic of India  
**Statutory Framework:** Central Goods and Services Tax Act 2017 (CGST), CGST Rules 2017, Companies Act 2013  

---

## 1. Key Statutory Constraints

### 1. Section 31 CGST Act & Rule 46 (Tax Invoice Requirements)
- **Consecutive Serial Numbering:** Tax invoices must contain a consecutive serial number, not exceeding 16 characters, in one or multiple series.
- **Allowed Character Set:** Alphabets, numerals, and special characters: hyphen/dash (`-`) and slash (`/`) only.
- **Annual Uniqueness:** The invoice series must be unique for a given financial year.
- **Mandatory Details:** Name, address, and GSTIN of supplier; recipient details; HSN code; taxable value; tax rate; and breakdown into CGST, SGST, and IGST.

### 2. Section 134 Companies Act 2013 (Financial Records & Approvals)
- Invoicing series, books of accounts, and financial signing authority must be approved by the Board of Directors or delegated to authorized officers (Finance Director, Principal Officer, or appointed Chartered Accountant).
- Fabrication or synthetic generation of audit credentials (`ca_audit_signed`) constitutes regulatory misrepresentation.
