## 1. Legal and Procedural Authenticity Assessment
- Under Section 179 of the Companies Act, 2013 and Secretarial Standard-1 (SS-1), a resolution of the Board of Directors becomes legally effective only when passed at a duly convened meeting with proper notice (Section 173(3)) and valid quorum (Section 174), and recorded in the minute books signed by the Chairman (Section 118).
- The documents in `artifacts/compliance/phase-4.8/board-submission/` represent draft proposals prepared by management, not executed minutes.
- Therefore, no authentic Board resolution exists. Engineering cannot treat draft proposals as enacted corporate authority.
