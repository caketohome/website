## 2. Operational Hours Clarification

The audit confirmed dual operating hours across all policies:
- **Customer Support Hours:** 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM IST daily.
- **Kitchen & Dispatch Cutoff:** 11:30 PM IST daily.
Both windows are distinct, complementary, and accurately represented.
