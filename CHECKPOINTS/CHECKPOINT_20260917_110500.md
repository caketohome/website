Root cause:
1. The shared footer (frontend/src/components/common/footer.tsx) had className="bg-ui-fg-base text-ui-fg-on-inverted". The class 'bg-ui-fg-base' does not exist in Tailwind CSS and evaluated to transparent background, while 'text-ui-fg-on-inverted' rendered white text on the cream (#FDFBF7) page background. This caused the 112px tall footer to be completely invisible, appearing as an unexplained empty space between the last section ("Behind the Scenes") and floating buttons.
2. The layouts (store)/layout.tsx and (checkout)/layout.tsx used 'min-h-[calc(100vh-8rem)]' on <main>, imposing an arbitrary 8rem deduction that conflicted with dynamic header and footer heights, causing artificial vertical stretching and scroll on short content.
3. Floating controls lacked env(safe-area-inset-bottom) offsets and overlapped the footer text on mobile devices without mobile-safe bottom padding.
