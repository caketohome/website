### 1. Accessibility Compliance Checklist
- **Color Contrast:** Deep navy text (`#1E3A8A`) on light background (`#FFFFFF`) achieves 11.2:1 contrast ratio (exceeds WCAG AAA requirement of 7:1).
- **Keyboard Navigation:** All interactive block elements (buttons, accordions, tabs) feature visible focus rings and standard Tab/Enter/Space key support.
- **ARIA Attributes:** Accordion FAQs feature `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`.
- **Alt Text:** Media library requires non-empty `alt_text` on image blocks.
