# CAKETOHOME — PHASE 6.2 PAGE TYPES & TEMPLATES SPECIFICATION
**Phase:** 6.2 — Workstream K  
**Author:** Product Analyst & UI/UX Architect  
**Status:** VERIFIED  

---

### 1. Supported Page Types
1. **`standard`**: General content page with rich text, headers, and image galleries.
2. **`landing`**: Conversion-focused marketing landing page with hero CTA, value propositions, and dynamic product showcases.
3. **`legal`**: Structured statutory policy page (Terms, Privacy, Refund, Shipping) with table of contents and last-updated stamp.
4. **`faq`**: Interactive accordion-based frequently asked questions page categorized by delivery, freshness, and payment.
5. **`contact`**: Interactive customer inquiry and branch location map page.
6. **`campaign`**: Time-limited holiday/promotional campaign page with countdown timer and promotional banner.

### 2. Standard Templates in `cms_page_template`
- `tmpl_home`: Homepage template (Hero, Category Strip, Featured Cakes, Value Proposition, Testimonials, Freshness Pledge).
- `tmpl_landing`: Marketing landing blueprint (Hero Banner, Product Grid, Trust Badges, CTA Form).
- `tmpl_legal`: Statutory document blueprint (Policy Header, Rich Text Section, Accordion Navigation).
- `tmpl_faq`: FAQ accordion blueprint (Categorized Accordions, Search Filter, Help Contact CTA).
